WE'VE ARRIVED!!! We made it to Barcelona. We are staying in the Eixample neighborhood. It's very posh and we super lucked out with our apartment.

We didn't waste any time. We got breakfast at a trendy spot next to our house. Then we walked around and explored the Gothic Quarter. The Barcelona Cathedral is amazing.
I couldn't believe my eyes when I saw it. I also made sure to have a lil nap. We stopped by the beach at sunset as well. 

Even after a horribly long journey over here, I felt immediately relaxed when we got here. What they say about slow living in Spain is true. And it is contagious. At all hours of the day and night we found people relaxing, eating, drinking, smoking and gossiping on park benches and in cafes.



I was lucky to discover the local's favorite drink on day one. Tinto de Verano which is red wine, sparkling water and lemon. I wish it didn't have alcohol so I could drink it all day. HeHe
