Day 2 had a rough start. We got up super early for our Sagrada Familia tour. When we got there, the teacher's union was barricading the entrance. This was a part of some massive movement going on here. A lot of teachers are on strike.

I always heard about the european striking lol. That is probably how they have such a high quality of living over here. Bc they don't put up w any bull.

So our cathedral tour was canceled, but it ended up being a great day regardless. We got breakfast at La Granota (shout out wolter's world on youtube lol). It's such a cute local breakfast spot. We ordered tortillas which are Spanish omelettes and they were FIREEEEE. I got one with brie and Jesse got the classic. We got two cafe con leches and our breakfast total was about 14 USD for both of us.

Then we toured this 600 year old hospital. Hospital de Sant Pau. This was crazy. It was so beautiful and the campus was huuuge. It def felt haunted to me. Wish we could've done a ghost tour. It was also raining cats and dogs outside. But I think that added to the vibes. And cleared out a ton of the crowds.

After that, Jesse had to start working (RIP. Thanks King). In the evening I took myself to a cello and piano concert downtown. I thought it was going to be at Palau de la Música Catalana which is a giant ornate music venue. Looks like Phantom of the Opera. But I didn't read my ticket and it was in a tiny music hall attached to the building :La

It was still a great concert and I was so glad I went anyway. I took myself to dinner at a bar near by in one of the alleyways. It reminded me of the izakayas in Japan. I really just ordered a plate of ham and a glass of tino de verano lol. Like Sam from iCarly or something. It was delicious of course.


Such a great day despite our tour getting canceled and the pouring rain.
